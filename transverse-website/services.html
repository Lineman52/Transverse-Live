Services

Four ways to work together, from a first look to full rollout. Most engagements start with the first one.

1. AI Readiness Assessment

A structured look at how your business actually operates — estimating, scheduling, invoicing, client communication — to find where AI would save real hours versus where it wouldn't help at all.

2. Tool Selection & Right-Sizing

Instead of chasing every new AI tool that comes up, I narrow the field to the two or three that fit your size, budget, and how your crew actually works.

3. Workflow Implementation

Getting a tool installed is the easy part. I help build it into your actual day-to-day process, so it's used, not ignored after week one.

4. Staff Training

Short, plain-English training sessions so your team is genuinely comfortable using what's been set up — not just aware it exists.

Not sure which one fits?

Most people start with the Readiness Assessment — it's the cheapest way to find out if there's a real opportunity before spending more.
